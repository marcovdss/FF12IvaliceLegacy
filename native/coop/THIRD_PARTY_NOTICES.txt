unmodified from the official release tarball (SHA-256 5f5993c530f084535c65a6879e9b26ad441169b3e25d789d83287040a9ca5165);
only the build configuration (third_party/sdl2/SDL_config.h) is ours. Recipe and pins: third_party/sdl2/.
